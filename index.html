OSINT.TOOLKIT

teaching-first · offline · no backend
Repo Contributing MIT

Self-contained tools for open source intelligence work. Every tool is a single HTML file: open it by double-click, it runs offline, and nothing you type ever leaves the page.

The goal is not just to automate lookups. It is to teach the method behind them, so you get faster and sharper at the actual work.

Tools

OSINT Learning Tool

Live
tools/OSINT-Learning-Tool.html

Teaching-first pivot console. Type any indicator — IP, domain, URL, email, username, hash, crypto wallet, person, phone, company, image, or topic — and it names what the indicator is, frames what you are actually trying to learn, and maps the sources worth checking with the reasoning behind each. Generates model-agnostic AI prompts in Learn, Pivot, Verify, and Report modes, extracts new indicators from pasted-back answers as clickable pivots, and builds a running case you can view as a node map and export to Markdown.

Teaches  where to look and why, passive vs. active sources, and how one indicator turns into the next.

Privacy and use.
  • Every tool here is a static HTML file. No backend, no network calls of its own, no accounts.
  • What you type stays in your browser. Favorites, notes, and cases use local browser storage.
  • AI prompts are copy-and-paste on purpose, so the tools stay model-agnostic and you control what goes to any AI.
  • Use these only against targets you are authorized to investigate, and follow the terms of service of the sources you visit.
