About

A single file that teaches OSINT by doing. Type one value: it names what you are holding, frames what you are trying to learn, hands you a model-agnostic AI prompt, and maps where to look and where to pivot, with the reasoning attached.

This is a launcher and a teacher, not a scraper. It never fetches data or touches a target. Links open in a new tab; the AI prompts are copy and paste into whatever model you use.

Save it locally and it remembers your favorites, notes, and any sources you add. Share it and each person builds their own copy.

Passive means you query a third party, not the target. Active means your action touches the target. Active sources are marked. Think before you run them.
